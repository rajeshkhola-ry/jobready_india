Exam Photos & Documents

3 tools

Photo HD Studio

Alt+P

Enhances, cleans, and resizes low-quality passport photos and documents to HD quality.

  1. Upload a photo.
  2. Clear or change the background.
  3. Set the required dimensions.
  4. Click Enhance to download the result.

Govt Exam Verifier

Alt+G

Checks your photo or signature against the exact size, pixel dimensions, and format required by government exam forms — SSC, UPSC, banking exams, and more.

  1. Upload your photo or signature.
  2. Select the target exam.
  3. The tool automatically resizes and compresses it to spec, so it isn't rejected at submission.

Doc Packager

Alt+D

Bundles every document a job application or exam needs — marksheet, ID, photo — into a single organized package.

  1. Select all your certificates and files.
  2. Arrange them in the order they should appear.
  3. Download the merged bundle.

Privacy & Security

2 tools

Privacy Masker

Alt+K

Blurs or blacks out sensitive details — Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID, and passport numbers — before you share a document. Also generates QR codes (URL, phone, email, WhatsApp) for posters and sharing.

  1. Upload the ID card or document.
  2. Pick the document type for an auto-applied redaction zone, or drag to draw your own mask.
  3. Save the masked file.

Fraud Seal / Watermark

Alt+F

Adds a "For Verification Only" label or a custom official seal/watermark to a document, so it can't be reused for something else.

  1. Upload the file.
  2. Choose a watermark text or seal.
  3. Adjust the opacity and apply.

PDF Toolkit

7 tools

PDF Editor & Tools

Alt+E

View a PDF, edit it, add text, and rearrange its pages.

  1. Drop in your PDF file.
  2. Select the page you want to remove or modify.
  3. Save the updated PDF.

PDF to Word

PDF Tools menu

Converts a PDF into an editable Word (.docx) file, preserving formatting, tables, and images.

  1. Open PDF Tools → PDF to Word.
  2. Upload your PDF.
  3. Download the converted .docx file.

PDF Compress

PDF Tools menu

Shrinks a PDF's file size for portals with an upload limit, while keeping it readable.

  1. Open PDF Tools → Compress.
  2. Upload your PDF (or say "Compress to 50 KB" using Voice Command on the homepage).
  3. Download the smaller file.

PDF Merge Tool

Alt+M

Combines two or more separate PDF files into a single PDF.

  1. Upload multiple PDF files.
  2. Drag to set their order.
  3. Click Merge.

PDF Split Tool

Alt+S

Extracts a page range or individual pages out of a larger PDF.

  1. Upload the PDF.
  2. Enter the page range you need (e.g. 1-3, 5).
  3. Download the extracted pages.

PDF Extract Tool

Alt+X

Pulls images or specific sections out of a PDF into a separate file.

  1. Upload the file.
  2. Click the image/data extract option.
  3. Download the result.

PDF Word Verifier

Alt+W

Cross-checks the text formatting, words, and readability inside a PDF.

  1. Upload the document.
  2. Run the automated text check.
  3. Confirm there are no hidden fonts or corrupted text before you submit it.

Career & Design

3 tools

AI Resume Builder

Alt+R

Builds an ATS-friendly, professional resume tailored to a job description.

  1. Fill in your details, or upload an existing resume.
  2. Choose from the AI-suggested bullet points.
  3. Select a template and download.

Poster & Banner Studio

Alt+B

Designs job recruitment ads, notices, and banners from ready-made templates, with Hindi/Devanagari font support. Also one-click resizes any design for Instagram, YouTube thumbnails, LinkedIn, Facebook, and X/Twitter.

  1. Select a template or a social-platform size.
  2. Edit the text and dates.
  3. Export instantly as a PNG or PDF.

CSV to Excel Converter

Alt+C

Converts tabular applicant-list data (CSV) into a clean Excel sheet.

  1. Drag and drop your .csv file.
  2. The tool prepares it as a readable .xlsx file, ready to download.
