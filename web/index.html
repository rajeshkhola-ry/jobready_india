Frequently Asked Questions

How do I resize a passport photo to SSC 200×230px and 50 KB?
Open the Govt Verifier tool, select the SSC Photo preset, upload your photo, and click Resize & Download. The tool automatically compresses the image to the highest quality within the 50 KB limit — 100% in your browser.
How do I add a Name and DOP strip to a passport photo?
Use the Name/DOP Strip tab in the Govt Verifier tool. Upload your photo, enter your name and date of photo, and download. The SSC/UPSC-standard black strip with white text is rendered at 3× resolution.
How do I add a purpose watermark to a document?
Open the Fraud Seal tool, upload your document image, type your purpose text and recipient, choose colour and opacity, then export. A diagonal watermark and authenticity seal are embedded locally.
How do I black out my Aadhaar number before sharing?
Use the Privacy Masker or Govt Verifier Smart Redactor. Select Aadhaar Front, upload the image, tap Auto-Apply Zones, and download. All redaction is 100% local — no file is uploaded.
How do I create a job application document bundle?
Open the Doc Packager, upload your Photo, Signature, Identity Proof, and Supporting Document. Each file is auto-optimised. Click Pack & Download ZIP to get a structured bundle in seconds.
Is my data safe? Are my documents uploaded anywhere?
No. All GetReadyJob tools process files entirely in your browser. No file, byte, or metadata is ever sent to any server. Your documents never leave your device.

Why mask ID numbers on resumes or job applications?
Masking hides sensitive digits while keeping the rest of the document readable, protecting you from identity theft and data leaks.
